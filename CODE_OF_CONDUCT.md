# Code of conduct

CommitGen is built for every developer, including beginners, people on old hardware and people writing in their second language. We want the project to feel welcoming to all of them.

## What we expect

- **Be kind and patient.** Everyone was new once. Answer questions the way you'd want yours answered.
- **Assume good intent.** Ask before assuming someone meant harm; text loses tone.
- **Critique code, not people.** Review comments explain what to change and why.
- **Respect people's time and choices.** A "no" to a feature request or a review suggestion is fine.
- **Make space.** Welcome contributions of every size, from typo fixes to new providers.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory jokes or language about anyone's identity, background or ability.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly pushing a point after being asked to stop.

## Where this applies

In the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, contact the maintainer privately through the contact details on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block people who break this code of conduct. The response depends on what happened: usually a private note first, a temporary or permanent ban for serious or repeated problems.

This code of conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
